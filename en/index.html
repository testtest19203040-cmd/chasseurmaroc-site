Hunting territories in Morocco

Seven types of land. One map.

The hunting plan built into the app tells each type of territory apart. Here is what it contains and what each colour means.

Association lots

1 550

Leased to a hunting association: access for members only, during the season.

Company lots

266

Leased to a hunting company, often for tourism: access with its permission.

Permanent reserves

223

Closed to hunting in every season, even when they overlap another lot.

Three-year reserves (Set B)

130

Closed for 2024-2027; Set A is open. The two sets alternate every three years.

Provincial lots

48

Managed at province level, open according to local conditions.

Rural properties

17

Private land where hunting depends on the owner.

Training lots

12

Reserved for hunting training and learning.

The plan helps you find your way and is not a hunting authorisation. Near a boundary, allow for GPS accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

Hunting in Morocco: the right questions.

When does hunting open in Morocco for the 2026-2027 season?

Under the 2026-2027 season decree, hunting for resident game and waterfowl opens on Sunday 4 October 2026. Partridge, hare and rabbit close on 3 January 2027, driven wild boar and waterfowl on 21 February 2027. Summer turtle dove hunting runs from 24 July to 6 September 2027.

Which days can you hunt in Morocco?

For most species, hunting is allowed on Sundays and national public holidays. Wild boar in a driven hunt authorised by ANEF can be hunted every day of the period, and turtle dove in summer on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

What are Set A and Set B of the hunting reserves?

Three-year reserves are split into two sets that alternate every three years. For 2024-2027, the 130 reserves of Set B are closed to hunting and Set A is open. The 223 permanent reserves stay closed in every season.

Does the app work without network?

Mostly yes: territory boundaries, season rules, your logbook and position are on the phone. Without network, the « Local » map shows territories and place names. Weather, community and account sign-in need internet.

Does the app replace my hunting permit?

No. Chasseur helps you read the hunting plan and the calendar. The hunting permit, insurance, membership of the managing association or company and the official texts remain mandatory.
